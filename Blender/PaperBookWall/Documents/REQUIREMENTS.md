## Core value

A minimal, bright reading room whose pale-oak book wall is the single hero subject, photographed
straight-on with calm symmetry.

## Confirmed requirements

### Purpose and audience

- [x] REQ-01: Deliver a single still image of a minimal modern reading room for a calm, editorial interiors look. The scene must read clearly at a glance with no clutter competing with the shelf wall.

### Visual outcome

- [x] REQ-02: Scene: minimal modern reading room; a pale-oak bookshelf wall is the hero, with white walls and a pale floor. The shelf wall is centered with white wall margin visible above it and at both sides.
- [x] REQ-03: Books: curated mixed arrangement. Mostly upright rows with varied heights, a few horizontal stacks and deliberate gaps; shelves must not look fully packed. Spine palette is paper-neutral (cream, kraft, off-white) with only faint muted accents.
- [x] REQ-04: Lighting and mood: bright airy daylight, soft shadows, calm and light overall.
- [x] REQ-05: Props: exactly one small potted plant and one horizontal book stack as accents. No other furniture.
- [x] REQ-06: Camera: frontal symmetrical composition, eye level with a slight low angle, shelf wall centered with breathing room on all sides.

### Scope and delivery

- [x] REQ-07: Deliver one 1920x1080 EEVEE hero render, the saved `paperbookwall.blend`, and review artifacts.

## Constraints

- Blender version: 5.2.
- File format / resolution: one PNG still at 1920x1080, EEVEE.
- Performance / delivery limits: single still frame; no animation.
- External assets: none. All geometry and materials built procedurally in-scene.

## Acceptance

- [x] REQ-08: Books are visibly shelved in rows and none float or intersect shelf boards, shelf sides, or each other.
- [x] REQ-09: Shelf geometry is clean, level, and regular, with no visible gaps or overlapping boards.
- [x] REQ-10: The composition is balanced and symmetrical, with clear white wall margin above and at both sides of the book wall.
- [x] REQ-11: Bright airy lighting reads clearly in the final render, with soft shadows and paper-neutral spines.

## Deferred

- None.

## Out of scope

- Animation and turntable clips: deliverable is one still.
- Extra camera angles or detail shots: deliverable is one hero render.
- Imported third-party assets: the scene is fully procedural.
- Furniture and large set dressing: would compete with the shelf wall hero.
