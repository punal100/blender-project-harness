## Core value

Minimal modern room corner with a wall shelf of books and one plant.

## Confirmed requirements

### Purpose and audience

- [ ] REQ-01: Deliver a clean, minimal modern interior scene that reads immediately as a room with books on a shelf.
- [ ] REQ-02: The scene is a single shelf-wall corner for portfolio/catalog presentation, not a full apartment.

### Visual outcome

- [ ] REQ-03: Style is minimal modern: neutral wall, light wood shelving, clean uncluttered geometry, restrained palette.
- [ ] REQ-04: Wall-mounted bookshelf with several rows of books; books vary in height, width, and muted colour.
- [ ] REQ-05: One small plant for depth; no other large furniture.
- [ ] REQ-06: Soft warm interior lighting with gentle shadows and a subtle floor material.
- [ ] REQ-07: Single camera in a three-quarter corner view framing the shelf and books clearly.
- [ ] REQ-08: Composition leaves breathing room around the shelf; no clutter or distracting background objects.

### Scope and delivery

- [ ] REQ-09: One 16:9 still render at 1920x1080 or larger, saved in `renders/final/`.
- [ ] REQ-10: Authoritative scene saved as `minimalbookwall.blend` at the project root.
- [ ] REQ-11: Project documents current: README, requirements, workflow, review, decisions, assets.
- [ ] REQ-12: Review passes with no genuine unresolved blocker or major finding.

## Constraints

- Blender version: 4.x as installed with this workspace.
- File format / resolution: `.blend` scene, PNG render, 16:9, 1920x1080 minimum.
- Performance / delivery limits: single still image; keep scene lightweight and renderable on local hardware.
- External assets: none planned; all geometry and materials authored in Blender.

## Deferred

- Animation or camera moves.
- Additional furniture or room layout beyond the shelf wall.
- Close-up detail renders.

## Out of scope

- Full room or apartment: user chose one shelf wall.
- Animation export: user chose a single camera still.
- Large furniture set: only one small plant is included for depth.
- External assets: not needed for this minimal scene.

- None.
