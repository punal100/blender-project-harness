# Design Decisions — Laundromat

Record decisions that affect the visual result, scope, assets, or delivery. Keep the reason and
impact, not a diary.

| Date | Decision | Reason | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | Project scaffold created. | New design project. | Persist exact requirements, workflow, and review records; fill README and goal before launch. |
| 2026-11-10 | Model everything with primitives and simple materials. | User chose no external assets, no generated media, and no scripts. | Realism comes from layout, geometry detail, material colour and roughness, and lighting. |
| 2026-11-10 | Interior-only room about 8 x 12 m with a 2.9 m ceiling. | User chose interior only; scale must be plausible for a coin laundromat. | Camera and aisle composition sized to the room; no exterior. |
| 2026-11-10 | Warm fluorescent ceiling panels plus cool daylight from the entrance. | Realistic laundromats mix interior fluorescents with storefront daylight. | Two light families; storefront glow behind the camera adds depth. |
| 2026-11-10 | Sage/teal machine faces with stainless trim and dark glass doors. | Requirements specify a teal or sage palette and simple materials. | Cohesive two-row colour scheme with reflective metal door rims. |
| 2026-11-10 | Twelve front-loading units, six per row, at 1.05 m spacing. | REQ-03 requires at least ten machines and two facing rows. | A full aisle read with believable laundromat density. |
| 2026-11-10 | Props grouped at the back wall: two folding tables, bench, vending, coin changer, clock, bins, cart, poster, and signage. | REQ-04 requires these props and the back wall was empty. | The aisle now terminates in a furnished, legible service area. |
| 2026-11-10 | Final polish: dark clock body with white hands, taupe table laminate, lighter door tint. | Full-spec review flagged a blank-looking clock and low-contrast tables. | Clock hands read at render distance; tables separate from the wall; doors read as tinted glass. |
| 2026-11-10 | Hero render delivered as `renders/final/R07-20261110T074103Z.png`. | Final sealed full-spec round. | Single 1920x1080 still with both rows, props, signage, and lighting readable. |
