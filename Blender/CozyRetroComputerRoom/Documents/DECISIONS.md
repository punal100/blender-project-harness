# Design Decisions — CozyRetroComputerRoom

Record decisions that affect the visual result, scope, assets, or delivery. Keep the reason and
impact, not a diary.

| Date | Decision | Reason | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-01 | Project scaffold created. | New design project. | Persist exact requirements, workflow, and review records; fill README and start the filled goal before launch. |
| 2026-11-01 | Hero camera is a tight 3/4 desk-corner close-up, 16:9. | User chose the close-up framing over wide room and window-seat options. | Room is built only to the depth the camera sees; background treated as soft bokeh. |
| 2026-11-01 | Beige CRT monitor plus separate PC tower is the focal hardware. | User selected this computer type. | CRT housing depth, bezel vents, tower vents, and yellowed-plastic wear are priority detail. |
| 2026-11-01 | All geometry and materials are authored procedurally; no external assets. | Keeps licensing simple and delivery self-contained. | No asset registration or packing; materials use procedural noise for wear. |
| 2026-11-01 | Cycles render at 1920x1080 with denoising. | Hero still quality with believable soft light and shallow depth of field. | Slower render inside the review flow but higher visual fidelity. |
